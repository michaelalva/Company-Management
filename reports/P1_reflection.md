## What Went Well
Our team, was able to divide reponsibilities across the different classes and split work evenly, while working on seperate parts on implementation and testing. And we focused on using proper github etiquette. 

## What Did Not Go Well
One challenge we faced was coordinating our schedules and finding meeting times when everyone could attend as some team members are online and others are in person students so schedules constantly clashed. Late communication sometimes happened later in the sprint which made it a bit difficult to coordinate changes and review work efficiently. 

## What To Do Differently
For future Sprints we could try to schedule meeting times better and earlier in the Sprint so  everyone is able to attend. We could also improve communication within the team about updating each other more frequently on progress and issues. And also starting to document everything earlier in the sprint would also help avoid rushing near the deadline.

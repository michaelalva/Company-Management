## What Went Well
During this sprint, our team continued dividing responsibilities effectively and working on different parts of the project at the same time. Although progress felt a bit slower durig certain points because of finals, exams, and other end of semester responsibilities, everyone still remained active and continued contributing to the project. Communication stayed consistent throughout the sprint, and team members were responsive whenever help or clarification was needed. Despite the heavier workload outside of class, we were still able to complete tasks efficiently and keep making steady progress. Compared to earlier sprints we had a better understanding of the project structure/workflow which helped reduce confusion and made collaboration smoother. Overall even with the added stress from finals week the team kept pushing through and worked together to complete the sprint successfully.

## What Did Not Go Well
A challenge our team faced during this sprint was staying fully organized with the timing and coordination of tasks. Because the sprint took place close to finals week everyone had a lot of outside responsibilities which caused us to start some parts of the sprint later than we originally planned. This sometimes led to confusion about what had already been completed and what still needed attention. Communication was still present throughout the sprint, but it could have been more proactive and clear in order to avoid confusion and reduce pressure on the team. 

## What To Do Differently
For future sprints we want to continue keeping tasks organized and completing work in a steady order like we did during this sprint. The team was able to finish most tasks on time before the demo. One area we could still improve on is communicating more clearly about which team member is responsible for each task so the workload is distributed more fairly. At times some members completed more tasks than others, so assigning responsibilities earlier and checking in more consistently would help balance contributions across the team. Continuing to improve communication and coordination will help make future sprints run even more smoothly and efficiently!
